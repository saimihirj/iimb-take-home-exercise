STARTUP SECTORS

Tamil Nadu is the second largest economy and its startup ranking is third in the country. Both time tested sectors and emerging sectors are driving the state’s startup economy.

In its pusuit to further improve the startup ecosystem and creating inclusive mass entrepreneurship in the state, TANSIM is proposing to work with sector-wise initiatives and policies.

The proposed scheme of things will include a friendly supportive governance ensuring a equal opportunity startup environment in the state.

DPIIT RECOGNITION

Get your recognized as Startup by DPIIT and get entitled for benefits such as grants, seed funding, market access, tax benefits, simplified compliance, ease of IPR and many more

Check Your Eligibility Criteria for DPIIT Startup Recognition

Age of Entity

Period of existence and operations should not be exceeding 10 years from the Date of Incorporation

Entity Type

Private Limited Company, a Registered Partnership Firm or a Limited Liability Partnership

Annual Turnover

Should have an annual turnover not exceeding Rs. 100 crore for any of the financial years since its Incorporation

Original Entity

Entity should not have been formed by splitting up or reconstructing an already existing business

Innovative & Scalable

Should work towards development or improvement of a product, process or service and/or have scalable business model with high potential for creation of wealth & employment
