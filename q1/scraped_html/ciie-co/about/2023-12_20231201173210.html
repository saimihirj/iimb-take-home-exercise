    Over the Years

    Set up at IIM Ahmedabad as a Centre of Excellence with support from the Government of India’s Department of Science and Technology and Government of Gujarat, CIIE.CO is the epicentre of The Innovation Continuum.

    1. 2002

      Innovation Centre at IIMA setup

    2. 2008

      Stay Hungry Stay Foolish published

    3. 2009

      Pioneering acceleration in India: iAccelerator

    4. 2010

      India’s largest B plan contest: The Power of Ideas

    5. 2011

      India’s first climate accelerator: Powerstart

    6. 2012

      India’s first energy VC: Infuse Ventures

    7. 2013

      Incubating Incubators

    8. 2014

      Foray into Regional Incubation: Startup Oasis, Rajasthan

    9. 2015

      India’s first Food & Agri-business accelerator launched

    10. 2016

      India’s first Healthcare accelerator launched

    11. 2017

      India Innovation Growth Program launched

    12. 2018

      Bharat Inclusion Initiative & Bharat Innovation Fund launched

    13. 2019

      Recognised as Centre of Excellence by DST, GoI

    14. 2021

      Expanded Regional Incubation: Assam & Madhya Pradesh

    15. 2022

      Startup Compass published

    16. 2023

      Bharat Seed Fund launched
