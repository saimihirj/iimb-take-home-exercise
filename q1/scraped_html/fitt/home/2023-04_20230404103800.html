ABOUT FITT

FITT is an industry interface organisation. It was established at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi) as a Registered Society on 9th July 1992. The mission of FITT is to be an effective interface with the Industry to foster, promote and sustain commercialization of Science and Technology in the Institute for mutual benefits.
