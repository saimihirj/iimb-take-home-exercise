Young Professionals Program (YPP)

Foundation for Innovation and Research in Science and Technology (FIRST), IIT Kanpur announces Young Professionals Program (YPP) to offer budding talent an opportunity in supporting incubation and innovation in the country and develop themselves into capable professionals. YPP extends exposure across functions and programs to potential young talent to assess their abilities, help them build skills, experiment with their capabilities, and craft their talent to emerge as competent professionals. The incumbents of the program acquire exposure to the following:

  • Outreach, social media, and pipeline
  • Applicant Empowerment, Support, and Evaluations
  • Innovation and Technology
  • Incubation and Portfolio Management

Program Benefits

  • The stint is challenging and expose participants to a variety of disciplines and business functions and provides exposure to a variety of businesses and functions.
  • The project/ activity allocations are based on business goals and the participants' interests, strengths, and development requirements.
  • Post-training, participants take on strategic or operational roles at junior management levels.

Compensation

  • A stipend of INR 25000 per month is offered during the one-year long YPP term.
  • After completion of 1 year the candidate(s) may get inducted as a full-time employee and can be promoted to the next level at a salary based on candidate's performance and industry norms, within the relevant department or function.

The Young Professionals Program (YPP) is an entry-point for an exciting journey at SIIC IIT Kanpur. Every year the incubator onboards a cohort of dynamic, fresh graduates with an aim to make them the leaders of tomorrow.

A YPP is exposed to the ongoing programs allowing the young talent to assess their abilities, help them nurture interdisciplinary skills, experiment with their capabilities, and craft their talent to emerge as competent professionals.

Eligibility

Graduate in Engineering/ Biotechnology/ Life Sciences/ Management/ Business Administration/ Development Studies/ Social Work/ Economics/ Commerce or any other relevant streams with <=2 years of professional experience can apply for the role of YPP.

What do we look for in YPP?

  • Proven aptitude for quick creative thinking
  • Excellent communication and coordination skills
  • Computer knowledge especially MS Office
  • Passion to serve the community
