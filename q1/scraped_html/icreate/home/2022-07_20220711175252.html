About - What is iCreate

Inspiring Innovation - Empowering Youth

Know More About Us

Winner of the National Award for Technology Business Incubator 2020, iCreate is India’s leading institution for transforming startups based on tech innovation into successful businesses. Started in 2012, iCreate is supported by the Government of Gujarat as well as the Government of India to facilitate ‘Next Generation Entrepreneurship’. This is not just in terms of encouraging technology startups, but also in empowering the Indian youth. It will lead to more youth pursuing their own dreams and converting them into successful ventures, instead of merely seeking jobs.

Our Ecosystem

Our ecosystem can be defined as an enabling environment that startups need, and it comprises key stakeholders such as corporates, government, investors and institutional partners. iCreate conducts international accelerator programmes which are aimed at getting world class innovation to address some of the big challenges faced by India. This could be through collaboration with leading institutions abroad, and exploring synergies between their startups and Indian industry. A good example of this is iCreate’s partnership with Israel’s Start-Up Nation Central (SNC), which resulted in the India Israel Innovation Accelerator (i3A). Going forward, international collaboration could also be with the intent of jointly exploring global markets. 

Corporate

iCreate helps companies fast-track innovation, with the power of collaboration. Established companies typically function in siloes and lack the agility of startups. We help them define their big challenges and match them with innovators, who are often part of the startup ecosystem. We go beyond providing connects, guiding the innovation across its journey – from an idea to a PoC to commercialization.

Government

Government plays a vital role in our ecosystem and is a champion of our vision to address India’s big challenges using innovation, and to empower the Indian youth with the ability to dream big and achieve those dreams. We work closely with the Government of Gujarat and the Government of India, and as a recognized Technology Business Incubator, we identify talented and passionate entrepreneurs, support them with incubation, and disburse various grants and funding schemes available from the government.

Investor

Investors are a critical link in a startup’s journey from an idea to a successful product/service, and then onwards as it scales up. At iCreate, we prepare entrepreneurs and innovators to become investor-ready. We do this by providing market connects, mentoring from domain experts, and guiding them in their strategic & financial planning.

Partner

iCreate has forged tie-ups with similar institutions and incubators, both from India and abroad. This enables us to tap into their resources, mentors, incubates and ideas, and help our entrepreneurs develop a global outlook – in ambition, reach and network. Such alliances also make it easy for us to collaborate in creating joint accelerator programmes with global scope.

How iCreate works

Our Approach to Supporting You

We follow what we call a ‘High-touch, Entrepreneur-first’ approach. This approach represents our philosophy as well as our differentiator.

Governance Entrepreneur First Approach

Governance

We recognize that vision, governance and execution, all play an important role in an institution’s success. Which is why we have put in place a robust, multi-tiered structure consisting of:

  • An Advisory Board – comprising eminent members of industry, academia and other fields – to provide the big picture and inspiration
  • A Statutory Board comprising senior technocrats, that ensures we have the right strategic direction and oversight
  • And a Team comprising of experts in various fields, who are committed to executional excellence

Entrepreneur First Approach

We follow what we call a ‘High-touch, Entrepreneur-first’ approach. ‘High-touch’ means we have dedicated startup lifecycle coaches who work with our startups, throughout the incubation process. ‘Entrepreneur-first’ means that we bet on an individual’s/team’s potential, rather than a specific idea. 

We understand that the best-laid plans do not always work out, so we support pivoting till a sustainable model is evolved. To enable the two components of our approach to come together seamlessly, we have designed a Startup Life Cycle Management process for efficient project selection and support. We provide a culture that stimulates independent thinking and does not stigmatise failure.
