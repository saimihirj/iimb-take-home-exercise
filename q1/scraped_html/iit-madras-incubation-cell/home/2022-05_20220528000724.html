IITM Incubation Cell coordinates & leverages the synergies in various strands of excellence driving innovation and entrepreneurship at IIT Madras, consisting of cutting edge research, industrial interactions, India’s first university-driven IITM Research Park and a stellar record of incubation in rural, social and industrial technologies.

Registered as a not-for-profit Section 8 Company, IITMIC is recognised as a Technology Business Incubator by Startup India, DIPP and NSTEDB, Dept of Science & Technology, Government of India. IITMIC supports Students, Faculty, Staff & Alumni of IITM and External entrepreneurs in creating successful deep Tech Startups, disrupting industries & translating benefits to the society at large.
