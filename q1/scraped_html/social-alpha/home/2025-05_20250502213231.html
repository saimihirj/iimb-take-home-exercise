Techtonic: Innovations for Agriculture and Rural Livelihoods
India’s agriculture, which supports half the nation’s population, is grappling with several challenges. Despite being the second-largest producer of agricultural products, India’s agricultural sector contributes only 17-18% to the nation’s GDP. Traditional practices, inadequate technological infrastructure, lack of financial support, and fragmented landholdings limit productivity, while dependence on unpredictable monsoons leaves farmers vulnerable. Recognising these issues, Techtonic: Innovations for Agriculture and Rural Livelihoods, designed in collaboration with NITI Aayog and PUSA, aims to identify and support groundbreaking technologies that address a curated set of challenges faced by smallholder farmers and enhance productivity, sustainability, and resilience in the agricultural sector.

Techtonic: Innovations in Sustainable Urban Transition

Increased urbanisation is linked to rising emissions. As per an estimate, cities and towns in India contribute two-thirds more emissions per person than in rural India. Since cities will increasingly play a role in India’s growth and development story, they will also be critical in helping India achieve net-zero emissions by 2070. Innovations in Sustainable Urban Transition, a joint initiative between Social Alpha and the U of T India Foundation in association with select urban local bodies, proposes to support innovators and entrepreneurs developing technology-led solutions to build climate resilience within urban geographies in India.
