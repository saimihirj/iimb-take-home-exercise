TECHNOLOGY INNOVATION FOR MID-SIZE ENTERPRISES
Welcome to SID. We were set up with a mission to ensure that scientific knowledge doesn’t lie dormant, but is brought to bear, in a manner that is useful to the industry and society at large. Here we strive to create and translate some of the finest original scientific knowledge and technology into a practical and valuable form in the real world. We approach this large and compelling task through three symbiotic entities that come together to compose SID.
