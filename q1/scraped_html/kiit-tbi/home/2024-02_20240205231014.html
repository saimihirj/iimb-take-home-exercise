International Programs

Enprendia

An Erasmus + co-funded capacity building which aims at enhancing entrepreneurial culture among female population in Indian HEIs.

Airothon

Airothon is a 2-day hackathon to find practical solutions for improving air quality. The hackathon will enable young minds to come with innovative indigenous ideas that will build a better tomorrow by taking proactive steps towards creating a carbon-negative environment.

RSN

Regional Startup Network aims to Build the entrepreneurial capacity in the Indo-pacific countries of Bangladesh, Bhutan, India and Nepal.

SEW

Social Entrepreneurship for women (SEW), initiative by the U.S. Consulate General- Hyderabad Office assists young women interested in social entrepreneurship.

SILICE

An Erasmus+ co-funded open-source platform that promotes social innovation ideas and international cooperation at regional level between the EU, Israel, and India.
